### `chladni` — Chladni / klankfiguren

- **parent theme:** `one-cymatics`

#### 1. `chladni-klang-sichtbar-1787`
- **register:** `documented`
- **label:** Chladni maakt klank zichtbaar
- **claim NL:** In 1787 publiceert Ernst Chladni een methode om elke klanksoort van vlakke klinkende lichamen niet alleen hoorbaar maar ook zichtbaar te maken.
- **quote:** "Da es mir gelungen ist, ein Mittel zu entdecken, um jede mögliche Art des Klanges solcher Körper ohne Beymischung anderer nicht nur hörbar, sondern auch sichtbar darzustellen; so hoffe ich, durch Bekanntmachung meiner Beobachtungen wenigstens einige richtige Voraussetzungen zu genauerer Untersuchung dieses noch sehr unbearbeiteten Theiles der Mechanik liefern zu können"
- **source:** Ernst Florens Friedrich Chladni — *Entdeckungen über die Theorie des Klanges* (1787) — https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/chladni_klang_1787/9
- **edge.because:** Dit is de historische kern van one-cymatics: trilling die vorm zichtbaar maakt, zonder uplink of netwerk.

#### 2. `chladni-violinbogen-platten-1787`
- **register:** `documented`
- **label:** Vioolboog op plaat en staaf
- **claim NL:** Chladni onderzoekt niet alleen snaren maar staven, blechstreifen, ringen, schijven en klokken door ze haaks met een vioolboog aan te strijken.
- **quote:** "An elastischen Stäben und Blechstreifen, wie auch an Ringen, Scheiben, Glocken und dergleichen klingenden Körpern, sind sowohl die Verhältnisse der verschiedenen Töne des nämlichen Körpers, als auch der gleichartigen Töne an verschiedenen Körpern sehr von denen unterschieden, die an Saiten Statt finden. … es wird also zu Untersuchung derselben besser seyn, den klingenden Körper unter einem rechten Winkel mit einem Violinbogen,"
- **source:** Ernst Florens Friedrich Chladni — *Entdeckungen über die Theorie des Klanges* (1787) — https://www.deutschestextarchiv.de/chladni_klang_1787/11
- **edge.because:** one-cymatics begint bij dit laboratoriumgebaar: boog + plaat als tegenpool van abstracte frequentie-hype.

#### 3. `chladni-feste-linien-figuren-1787`
- **register:** `documented`
- **label:** Vaste lijnen tekenen de figuur
- **claim NL:** Chladni beschrijft hoe “feste Linien” op klinkende lichamen zich verbinden, kruisen en buigen tot wisselende figuren, terwijl de toon hetzelfde blijft.
- **quote:** "Manche Figuren werden bisweilen dadurch so sehr verändert, daß man ohne gehöriger Uebung ihre eigenthümliche Gestalt nicht würde daraus beurtheilen können. … Der Ton ist bey einer abgeänderten Figur der nämliche, als wenn sich die Figur ganz regelmäßig zeigt. … öfters werden selbige auch dadurch veranlaßt, wenn man den klingenden Körper nicht genau an den Stellen hält oder berührt, wo eigentlich feste Linien seyn sollten"

### `jenny` — Hans Jenny / cymatics

- **parent theme:** `one-cymatics`

#### 1. `jenny-cymatics-named-1967`
- **register:** `documented`
- **label:** Jenny noemt het veld cymatics
- **claim NL:** Hans Jenny kiest de naam cymatics (Grieks ta kymatika) voor empirisch onderzoek naar effecten van golven/trillingen, niet voor hypothesen over eindoorzaken.
- **quote:** "If a name is required for this field of research it might be called cymatics (to kyma, the wave; ta kymatika, matters pertaining to waves, wave matters). This underlines that we are not dealing with vibratory phenomena in the narrow sense, but rather with the effects of vibrations. Our documentation is primarily concerned with the experimental demonstration of phenomena in the acoustic and lower ultrasonic range."
- **source:** Hans Jenny — *Cymatics — The structure and dynamics of waves and vibrations* (Vol. I) (1967; MACROmedia 2001) — https://monoskop.org/images/3/31/Jenny_Hans_Cymatics_A_Study_of_Wave_Phenomena_and_Vibration_Vol_1_2001.pdf

#### 2. `jenny-triadic-phenomenon`

- **quote:** "Since the various aspects of these phenomena are due to vibration, we are confronted with a spectrum which reveals patterned, figurate formations at one pole and kinetic-dynamic processes at the other, the whole being generated and sustained by its essential periodicity. … The three fields — the periodic as the fundamental field with the two poles of figure and dynamics — invariably appear as one."
- **source:** Hans Jenny — *Cymatics — The Basic Triadic Phenomenon* (1967/2001) — https://monoskop.org/images/3/31/Jenny_Hans_Cymatics_A_Study_of_Wave_Phenomena_and_Vibration_Vol_1_2001.pdf

### `resonance` — Resonantie (Helmholtz)

- **parent theme:** `one-cymatics`

#### 1. `helmholtz-sympathetic-resonance`
- **register:** `documented`
- **label:** Helmholtz: meetrilling is mechanisch
- **claim NL:** Helmholtz toont dat sympathetische resonantie objectieve mechanische effecten heeft, onafhankelijk van het oor: periodieke impulsen met passende periode bouwen grote oscillaties op.
- **quote:** "Such an effect occurs in the phenomenon of sympathetic resonance. This phenomenon is always found in those bodies which when once set in motion by any impulse, continue to perform a long series of vibrations before they come to rest. When these bodies are struck gently, but periodically, although each blow may be separately quite insufficient to produce a sensible motion in the vibratory body, yet, provided the periodic time of the gentle blows is precisely the same as the periodic time of the body's own vibrations, very large and powerful oscillations may result."
- **source:** Hermann von Helmholtz (trans. A.J. Ellis) — *On the Sensations of Tone*, Chapter III (1895 Ellis ed.) — https://sensationsoftone.com/Chapter3.html
- **edge.because:** Resonantie is hier fysica, geen 6G-mythe — one-cymatics leunt op dit mechanische feit.

#### 2. `helmholtz-violin-unison`
- **register:** `documented`
- **label:** Twee violen, één snaar beweegt mee
- **claim NL:** Helmholtz noemt het muzikanten-bekende geval: snaren van twee violen in unisono — strijk de ene, de andere begint mee te trillen.
- **quote:** "Musicians are well acquainted with sympathetic resonance. When, for example, the strings of two violins are in exact unison, and one string is bowed, the other will begin to vibrate."
- **source:** Hermann von Helmholtz — *On the Sensations of Tone*, Chapter III (1895) — https://sensationsoftone.com/Chapter3.html
- **edge.because:** Meetrilling tussen snaren is het alledaagse bewijs dat one-cymatics aan resonantie hangt.

#### 3. `helmholtz-chladni-sand-nodes`
- **register:** `documented`
- **label:** Zand verzamelt zich op knopen
- **claim NL:** Helmholtz documenteert Chladni’s methode: strooi zand op een trillende membraan/plaat — zand verzamelt zich op knooplijnen (nodal lines).
- **quote:** "By strewing sand on the membrane the figures are easily rendered visible, for as soon as it begins to vibrate the particles of sand collect on the nodal lines. … In the same way it is possible to render visible the nodal lines and forms of vibration of oval and square membranes, and of differently-shaped plane elastic plates, bars, and so on. These form a series of very interesting phenomena discovered by Chladni"
- **source:** Hermann von Helmholtz — *On the Sensations of Tone*, Chapter III (1895) — https://sensationsoftone.com/Chapter3.html
- **edge.because:** Resonantie + Chladni-zand is de schoolbank-brug van one-cymatics naar zichtbare trilling.

- **quote:** "A mechanical oscillator drives a string held under tension by a mass hanging over a pulley. As the frequency of the driver is increased, the number of antinodes increases."
- **source:** University of Minnesota School of Physics and Astronomy Lecture Demonstrations — *3B22.10 Melde's Standing Wave Machine* (n.d.; geraadpleegd 2026) — https://spademos.umn.edu/3B22.10

Specifically, the length of the string must be some integral number of half-wavelengths for the initial running wave. … the system is said to be in resonance"
- **source:** Oklahoma Christian University Physics Laboratory — *STANDING WAVES IN A STRING: MELDE’S EXPERIMENT* (n.d.; geraadpleegd 2026) — https://classes.oc.edu/PhysicsLab/WAVES.htm

### `exclusion-zone` — Exclusion zone / EZ-water (Pollack)

- **parent theme:** `one-water`

#### 1. `zheng-pollack-100um-2003`
- **register:** `documented`
- **label:** Microsferen weg tot ~100 µm
- **claim NL:** Zheng & Pollack (2003) meten dat microsferen worden uitgesloten tot zones in de orde van 100 µm vanaf hydrofiele geloppervlakken — verder dan conventionele theorie voorspelt.
- **quote:** "Aqueous suspensions of microspheres were infused around gels of varying composition. The solutes were excluded from zones on the order of 100 µm from the gel surface. We present evidence that this finding is not an artifact, and that solute-repulsion forces exist at distances far greater than conventional theory predicts."
- **source:** Jian-ming Zheng & Gerald H. Pollack — *Long-range forces extending from polymer-gel surfaces* (Phys. Rev. E; arXiv preprint) (2003) — https://arxiv.org/pdf/cond-mat/0305093
- **edge.because:** EZ-uitsluiting is de primaire laboratoriumbrug van one-water naar levend/geordend water.

#### 2. `pollacklab-fourth-phase-claim`
- **register:** `in-development`
- **label:** Pollack: vierde fase aan interfaces
- **claim NL:** Pollack Lab claimt dat EZ-water een fysisch onderscheiden, geordende “vierde fase” is (vloeibaar-kristal-achtig), gevoed door licht/IR — mechanisme blijft onder studie.
- **quote:** "Water has three phases – gas, liquid, and solid; but findings from our laboratory imply the presence of a surprisingly extensive fourth phase that occurs at interfaces. The formal name for this fourth phase is exclusion-zone water, aka EZ water. … NMR, infrared, and birefringence imaging, as well as measurements of electrical potential, viscosity, and UV-VIS and infrared-absorption spectra, collectively reveal that the solute-free zone is a physically distinct, ordered phase of water. It is much like a liquid crystal."
- **source:** Gerald H. Pollack / Pollack Laboratory — *Research — EZ Water* (n.d.; lab site; book Fourth Phase 2013 genoemd) — https://www.pollacklab.org/research
- **edge.because:** De fourth-phase-hypothese voedt one-water, maar blijft open — daarom in-development, niet als wet.

#### 3. `pollack-ir-ez-expansion-2021`
- **register:** `documented`
- **label:** Mid-IR vergroot de EZ
- **claim NL:** Pollack-groep (2021): 5 min mid-IR vergroot EZ bij Nafion met expansieratio ~1,41 en drijft tegelijk protonen naar bulk (PZ).
- **quote:** "After irradiation for 5 min by mid-IR light, EZ size increased with an expansion ratio of 1.41. A significant expansion was also found in the size of PZ, equivalent to an increment of about 1.39 × 10¹⁶ molecules of hydronium ions. Thus, IR radiation not only builds EZ, but at the same time drives release of protons from the growing EZ to bulk water."
- **source:** Pollack Laboratory (paper summary) — *Effect of infrared radiation on interfacial water at hydrophilic surfaces* (2021) — https://www.pollacklab.org/single-post/effect-of-infrared-radiation-on-interfacial-water-at-hydrophilic-surfaces

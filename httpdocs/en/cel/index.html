cell

Cell is a scrolling wall of fourteen dark plates about the cell, in the style of Haeckel. Darwin saw a small pool of mud; we now know a whole universe lives in it. Move a magnifying glass over the mud, tap the sodium-potassium pump, let kinesin walk along its threads and see how big a cell is when you blow it up to the size of a city. Every plate has one thing to touch, and ideas that are still disputed are called disputed on the plate itself.
